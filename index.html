A Bit About Me

I'm currently diving into the practical side of things as an Article Trainee at SAR Associates, a firm of Chartered Accountants, since February 2025. Before that, I had a fun run as President of the GCM Social Club from 2022 to 2023. We organized engaging events like chats on youth economy with MP Gagan Thapa and Economist Dr. YubaRaj Khatiwada, plus a session on socio-economic issues with Ex-Governor Dr. Chiranjibi Nepal. We also ran blood donations with the Nepal Red Cross Society and pitched in for the Bagmati Cleanliness Program.
